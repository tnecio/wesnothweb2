(COPYING.txt in this directory). Source code: https://github.com/tnecio/wesnothweb2

The game content it plays -- campaigns, units, maps, images, music and sounds -- is The Battle for
Wesnoth's, taken unmodified from the upstream project (https://github.com/wesnoth/wesnoth) and
distributed under its terms: the GNU General Public License, version 2 or later, except where
wesnoth-copyrights.csv lists another licence (for example CC BY-SA 4.0) and the authors for a file.
Sound effects stored as WAV upstream are served re-encoded as Ogg Vorbis; nothing else is altered.
